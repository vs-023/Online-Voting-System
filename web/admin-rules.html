Election Administration Rules

These rules and guidelines must be followed by all administrators to ensure secure, transparent and fair election management within the Online Voting System.

1. Secure Admin Access

Admin credentials must remain confidential. Unauthorized sharing of login details is strictly prohibited.

2. Candidate Verification

All candidate information must be verified properly before approval into the election system.

3. Fair Election Management

Administrators must maintain neutrality and fairness throughout the voting and result declaration process.

4. Voter Data Protection

Voter personal information must remain protected and should never be disclosed publicly.

5. Request Handling

Correction requests from voters should be reviewed carefully and processed only after proper verification.

6. Election Monitoring

Admins are responsible for monitoring voting activities and identifying suspicious or duplicate voting attempts.

7. Result Transparency

Election results must be generated accurately without manipulation or unauthorized modifications.

8. System Security

Maintain database security, backup records regularly, and prevent unauthorized system access.

Important Notice: Any misuse of administrator privileges, unauthorized data modification, or violation of election integrity rules may result in permanent removal of admin access and legal action.
