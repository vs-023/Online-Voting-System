Voter Eligibility Criteria

The Online Voting System ensures that only eligible citizens participate in elections. Every voter must satisfy certain conditions and provide valid identification details before accessing the voting portal.

Basic Eligibility Requirements

  • The voter must be a citizen of the country.
  • The voter must be at least 18 years old.
  • A valid government identification document is required.
  • The voter must complete successful registration.
  • Each voter can cast only one vote during an election.

Required Documents

  • National Identity Card / Aadhaar Card
  • Valid Email Address
  • Registered Mobile Number
  • Recent Passport Size Photograph (optional)

Security & Verification

  • All voter details are verified before approval.
  • Duplicate registrations are automatically rejected.
  • User passwords are securely protected.
  • Votes remain anonymous and confidential.

Important Notice

Providing false information during registration may lead to cancellation of voter access and disqualification from the election process.
