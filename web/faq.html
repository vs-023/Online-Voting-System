Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register to vote?
You can register using the Register page by providing your valid details such as name, ID, and email.
2. Who is eligible to vote?
Any citizen who meets the eligibility criteria defined in the Eligibility section can vote.
3. Is online voting secure?
Yes, the system uses authentication and secure database storage to ensure safety and privacy.
4. Can I change my vote after submitting?
No, once submitted, votes cannot be changed to maintain election integrity.
5. What if I forget my login password?
You can reset your password using the recovery option on the login page.
