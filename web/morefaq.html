More Frequently Asked Questions

How is my vote counted?
Votes are securely stored in the database and counted automatically after election ends.
Can I vote from mobile?
Yes, the system is fully responsive and works on mobile devices.
What happens if the server crashes?
Backup systems ensure that all votes are safely stored and recovered.
Is my identity visible?
No, voting is completely anonymous.
