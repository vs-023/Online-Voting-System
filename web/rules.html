Rules Before Casting Vote

Please read all voting rules carefully before submitting your vote. Once the vote is submitted, it cannot be changed or modified.

1. One Person One Vote

Every registered voter is allowed to cast only one vote. Multiple voting attempts are strictly prohibited.

2. Verify Candidate Carefully

Read all candidate details properly before selecting your preferred candidate.

3. Vote Cannot Be Changed

Once your vote is submitted successfully, it cannot be edited or cancelled.

4. Maintain Privacy

Do not share your login credentials or voting details with anyone.

5. Use Valid Information

Any false information or misuse of the system may lead to account suspension.

6. Secure Internet Connection

Always vote using a secure and trusted internet connection for safe voting experience.

Important: After clicking the "Submit Vote" button, your vote will be permanently stored in the database. Please ensure your selected candidate is correct.
